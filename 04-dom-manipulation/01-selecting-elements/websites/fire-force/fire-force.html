About the Anime

Fire Force takes place in a world where mysterious human combustion causes people to spontaneously burst into flames. The story follows Shinra Kusakabe, a young firefighter with the ability to control fire with his feet, as he joins Special Fire Force Company 8 and investigates the mysterious events behind the human combustion phenomenon.
