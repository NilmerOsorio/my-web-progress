About the Anime

Tokyo Ghoul follows Ken Kaneki, a university student whose life changes after a chance encounter with a mysterious girl. After a tragic incident, Kaneki becomes a half-ghoul and must learn to survive between the human and ghoul worlds while struggling with his new identity.

Information
