About the Anime

My Hero Academia is a superhero anime set in a world where most people are born with special abilities known as Quirks. The story follows Izuku Midoriya, a boy who dreams of becoming a hero despite being born without a Quirk.

Information

  • Author: Kohei Horikoshi
  • Studio: Bones
  • Genres: Action, Superhero, Shonen
  • First aired: 2016
  • Status: Finished
