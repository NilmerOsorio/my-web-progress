About the Anime

Bleach follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who gains the powers of a Soul Reaper after encountering Rukia Kuchiki. From that moment, Ichigo becomes responsible for protecting humans from dangerous spirits known as Hollows while uncovering the mysteries surrounding the Soul Society.
