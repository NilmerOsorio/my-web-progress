About the Anime

Darling in the Franxx takes place in a dystopian future where humanity lives inside fortified settlements and children are trained to pilot giant robots called Franxx. The story follows Hiro, a young pilot who meets Zero Two, a mysterious girl with horns, and becomes her partner in the fight against the terrifying creatures known as Klaxosaurs.
